AMEE 2025 Full Programme: Plan Your Conference Journey

The main conference begins with the Opening Ceremony on Sunday, August 24th, and continues until the Closing Ceremony on Wednesday, August 27th.
Note: This is a provisional programme and may be subject to change. The final programme will be confirmed and released by the end of July.

23

August 23 (Saturday): Pre-conference Workshop Day

All-day registration (08:00-17:30).
Morning and afternoon pre-conference workshops (topics include Systematic Review, AI Ethics Integration, etc).
AI Forum Day 1: Innovative AI!
If you are not registered for workshops, attendance is not required.

24

August 24 (Sunday): Pre-conference Workshops & Welcome Reception

All-day registration (08:00-21:30).
AI Forum Day 2: The Next Step of AI!
ESME Masterclass: Introduction to Health Professions Education Research.
More pre-conference workshops.
Highlight: First-time Attendees Orientation (16:30-17:15).
✨ Welcome Reception ✨ (19:45-21:15) - A great networking opportunity!

25

August 25 (Monday): Main Conference Day

Registration (07:30-17:30).
Multiple parallel sessions (Symposia, Research Papers, Workshops, PechaKucha, etc).
Highlights: Keynote (Prof. Adam Rodman) and ASPIRE-to-Excellence Awards (11:00-12:30).
Coffee break & exhibition, lunch & exhibition.

AMEE Taiwan Night Networking Dinner
Time: 19:00-22:00
Venue: Barceló Sants Hotel (Barcelona, near city center and connected to train, metro, and airport line).

26

August 26 (Tuesday): Main Conference Day

Registration (07:30-17:30).
More parallel sessions.
Highlights: Patil Teaching Innovation Award.
Coffee break & exhibition, lunch & exhibition.

27

August 27 (Wednesday): Closing Day

Morning registration (07:30-13:00).
Morning parallel sessions.
Highlights: Keynote (Prof. Champion N. Nyoni, WHO), Conference Awards & AMEE 2025 Closing Ceremony (11:00-12:30).
Coffee break & final exhibition.
